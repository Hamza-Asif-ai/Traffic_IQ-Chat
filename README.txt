Put a short traffic/road video here (e.g. traffic_sample.mp4) to test the
notebook and process_video.py. A 10-30 second clip filmed from a fixed,
slightly elevated angle (overhead or CCTV-style) works best for both
detection accuracy and speed calibration.

Free sample traffic footage can be found on sites like Pexels or Pixabay
(search "traffic drone" or "highway overhead") if you don't have your own.
